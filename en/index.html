Neighbourhood

Wrocław at your fingertips

You live between the river and the Old Town. A morning walk along the boulevards, coffee in Nadodrze at noon, a concert or dinner on the Market Square in the evening – all without a car.

Right on the Oder

The building stands on the embankment and promenade – perfect for a morning jog, a bike ride or an evening stroll at sunset. Wrocław is a city of islands and more than a hundred bridges, and in summer cruise boats, kayaks and pedal boats set off from the marinas.

Market Square and Old Town

About a kilometre to one of the most beautiful market squares in Europe: the Gothic Town Hall, colourful townhouses, Salt Square with its flower stalls and hundreds of Wrocław dwarfs to discover.

Culture

National Forum of Music, Capitol Musical Theatre, Wrocław Opera and dozens of galleries. The city that was European Capital of Culture 2016 is full of festivals all year round.

Coffee, food, Nadodrze

Restaurants and cafés 70 m from the door. A few minutes away: atmospheric Nadodrze and Ruska Street with Pokoyhof Passage and Neon Side Gallery – where Wrocław meets in the evenings.

Landmarks and walks

Ostrów Tumski with its cathedral and the lamplighter who lights the gas lamps, the University's Aula Leopoldina, the City Arsenal. The oldest part of the city starts practically around the corner.

Easy everyday life

Żabka and a bakery on the ground floor, trams and buses from Jana Pawła II Square to every part of the city, city bike stations and riverside paths. A car becomes an extra, not a necessity.

Why is this place special?

  1. See the city and the river from an exceptional height. The west-facing view from a high floor opens up a stunning panorama of the city and the river. A beauty you can enjoy every day – watching the afternoon sun slowly give way to picturesque sunsets.
  2. City centre without the bustle. The Market Square is a short walk away, yet you enjoy the calm of the embankment, greenery and the residents' terrace.
  3. A location that holds its value. Downtown on the Oder, close to universities and offices – an address that is always in demand, both to live in and to rent out.
