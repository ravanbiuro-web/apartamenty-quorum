About the apartment

For sale: an apartment in the centre of Wrocław, on the top, 23rd floor of the Quorum Apartments tower – right on the Oder embankment and its beautiful promenade. Living at this height is a real rarity in Wrocław: only three buildings in the whole city offer apartments this high.

The apartment is fully finished and furnished – ready to move in right away. A separate dining area with a round table, a bathroom with a bathtub and west-facing windows overlooking the city skyline, the Oder and Quorum Tower. The price includes all furniture and the appliances shown in the photos.
