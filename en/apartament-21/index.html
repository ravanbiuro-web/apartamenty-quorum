About the apartment

For sale: an apartment in the centre of Wrocław, on the 21st floor of the Quorum Apartments tower – right on the Oder embankment and its beautiful promenade. Living at this height is a real rarity in Wrocław: only three buildings in the whole city offer apartments this high.

The apartment is fully finished and furnished – ready to move in right away. You save time and avoid the stress of a long renovation, choosing materials and supervising contractors. West-facing windows look out over the city skyline, the Oder and Quorum Tower. The price includes all furniture and the appliances shown in the photos.
